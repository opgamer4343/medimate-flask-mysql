About MediMate

school B.S. Biomedical Computing / Capstone Design Thinking Project

MediMate is a smart medicine reminder dashboard designed as a college software and Design Thinking project to make medication routines easier to organize and track. It brings medicine schedules, timely reminders, medication history logs, and adherence reports together into a calm, accessible interface.

Developed under human-centered design principles, this project addresses common compliance hurdles faced by students, elderly patients, and busy caregivers. By eschewing confusing medical jargon and cognitive overload, the system maintains strict fidelity to authentic pharmaceutical charting metaphors.
